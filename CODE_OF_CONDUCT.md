# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Persona Voice to be safe, respectful, and technically honest for
people of every background and experience level. Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and users
are expected to help create an environment free from harassment and intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas, evidence, and code rather than people.
- State uncertainty and platform limitations plainly.
- Welcome corrections and give credit for others' work.
- Protect private audio, logs, identities, security reports, and target-voice consent.
- Respect copyright, licenses, trademarks, and community terms.
- Accept reasonable maintainer direction on scope, safety, and review process.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, deliberate humiliation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information, audio, credentials, or communications without
  explicit permission.
- Submitting or promoting unauthorized impersonation/voice material.
- Pressuring someone to weaken a safety, privacy, consent, or licensing boundary.
- Retaliation against a good-faith report.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project-linked
community spaces, and public representation of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a non-sensitive concern, contact the maintainers through a focused repository issue. For a
confidential conduct or security/privacy concern, use the private reporting path in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and identify it as a conduct report. If repository maintainers are
involved or that path is unavailable, use GitHub's platform abuse/reporting tools.

Maintainers may edit/remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, issue warnings,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation when behavior threatens the
community or project. Enforcement should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety
of affected people. Report details should be shared only as needed to investigate and act.

Good-faith disagreement or a technical mistake is not misconduct. Repeated bad-faith behavior,
concealment, or refusal to respect boundaries may be.
